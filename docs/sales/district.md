# District buyer brief

For instruction, technology and procurement leaders serving ages 3-15,
Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the UI has 8 languages.

## Proposed use

Evaluate supplemental English reading practice at selected sites before deciding
whether to expand. The current family reader offers AI stories, narration, word
help, quizzes and adult progress views. It does not replace the district's core
curriculum or established assessments. Review content and device accessibility
against local requirements before selecting a cohort.

## What district leaders should expect

**Planned / PR #130:** teachers enter reading sessions and dated assessments;
school administrators review their own school's classes and teachers. CSV roster
import is implemented on that branch. Remote roster adapters require existing
authorized connections and live verification. Neither student SSO nor automatic
family-to-school activity ingestion is included. The branch is not merged or
production-verified.

The tenancy boundary is a school. A central district dashboard, cross-school
consolidated reporting, district role hierarchy and district-wide provisioning
are not included in that release. A multi-school evaluation needs a separately
scoped operating plan; do not promise central access to all school records.

## Proposed buying process

Name an instructional sponsor, a school coordinator for each site, and technology,
privacy and procurement reviewers. Agree a limited cohort and staff recording
workload. Complete the [privacy review](privacy-security.md) and deployment gates
before any real roster. The [timeline](implementation.md) is conditional on those
decisions, not a guaranteed launch date.

The [pricing proposal](pricing-proposal.md) starts at $10,000/month; networks with
more readers or schools move into larger tiers. Require explicit allowances,
service scope and exit terms rather than treating the consumer subscription as
a district license.

## Evaluation decision

Use the [measurement plan](outcomes-measurement.md) to agree participation,
recording completeness and dated observations. Compare the same readers over
time, disclose missing observations and consider the district's independent
assessment evidence. There are no district customers, testimonials or efficacy
statistics offered in this packet. [Capability evidence](capabilities.md).
