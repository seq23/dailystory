# Institutional pricing proposal

**Proposal only — not approved prices, a quote, an active checkout plan or a
committed service package.** Intended for programs serving ages 3-15,
Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the UI has 8 languages.

## Proposed annual contract bands

| Proposed tier | Eligible enrolled readers | Participating sites | Monthly contract | Annual equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Institutional program | Up to 1,000 | Up to 5 | $10,000 | $120,000 |
| Institutional network | Up to 3,000 | Up to 15 | $20,000 | $240,000 |
| Large network | Up to 8,000 | Up to 40 | $40,000 | $480,000 |
| Enterprise | Above 8,000 or 40 sites | Custom | From $60,000 | From $720,000 |

Use the smallest band that covers **both** enrollment and sites. Thus 800 readers
at 10 sites need the network band; 4,000 readers at 4 sites need large network.
A site is one participating school, branch or program location. Count eligible
readers rather than monthly logins; agree deduplication and count review in the
contract. Every tier is a proposed ceiling for commercial scoping, not tested
system capacity. Enterprise pricing increases with scope; it is not unlimited.

## Why these bands

The $10,000/month floor positions the offer as a managed institutional program
that must fund onboarding, privacy review, training and ongoing support, subject
to delivery planning. Enrollment and site count both increase operating work.
At full enrollment, the first three bands imply $120, $80 and $60 per reader per
year respectively: an intentional scale discount while total contracts grow.
At lower utilization the effective per-reader cost is higher. These are pricing
design choices, not market benchmarks, verified margins or savings claims.

The proposed service scope is a readiness workshop, an onboarding plan, staff
orientation and scheduled program reviews. Staffing capacity and frequency must
be confirmed before inclusion in an order. Each order must separately specify
story generation and narration allowances, concurrency, account entitlements,
support hours, escalation, rollout sites and any setup work. Model actual usage
and delivery costs privately before quoting. Do not promise unlimited inference,
premium narration availability, a service-level agreement or new integrations.

## Evaluation and contract terms to resolve

Proposal: a paid, fixed-scope evaluation at the applicable monthly band after
readiness gates pass, with its own duration, cohort, usage caps, acceptance and
exit terms. Any credit toward an annual contract requires written agreement;
there is no automatic renewal or purchase obligation assumed here.

Annual totals above are 12 times monthly amounts, with no annual discount assumed.
Taxes, setup fees, payment schedule, overage handling, renewal, cancellation,
data return/deletion and purchase-order/invoice arrangements remain to be agreed.
Do not start billing or purchase services based on this document.

## Product boundary

[Family pricing](../PRICING.md) describes the implemented consumer checkout.
It does not grant institutional seats. Institutional billing, entitlement
provisioning at this scale, district consolidation and library adaptations are
not implemented. **Planned / PR #130** adds school records, not commercial plans.
Use [capability evidence](capabilities.md), [implementation gates](implementation.md)
and [privacy review](privacy-security.md) to establish a deliverable scope first.
