# Outcomes and measurement

For ages 3-15, Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the UI has 8 languages.
This is a proposed evaluation method, not a report of measured customer outcomes.

## What can be observed

The current family reader records reading sessions and quiz attempts for parent
views. Recorded sessions, elapsed minutes and quiz results describe app activity;
they do not establish attentive reading, independent proficiency or causal gains.
Vocabulary and saved-story totals in the child progress hook are account-level,
so they must not be presented as uniquely attributable to an individual child.

**Planned / PR #130:** school reports use teacher-entered minutes, completed
sessions, dates, School grade, Reads at and correct/total results for vocabulary,
spelling, definitions, context and inference. They do not automatically ingest
family reading or quizzes. A repeated completed session counts again; it is not
a distinct book. Imported grades do not manufacture a historical baseline.

## Proposed evaluation scorecard

| Question | Measure and denominator | Collection / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the program reach its cohort? | Participating readers / eligible readers over an agreed window | Staff-maintained attendance; not an automated institution report |
| Was the routine used? | Recorded completed sessions and total recorded minutes | Family app activity or planned teacher records; label the source and keep them separate |
| Is the evidence complete? | Readers with two dated levels / total enrolled readers | Planned report coverage; disclose absent observations |
| Did observations change? | First/latest dated reading level for the same child | Planned report; grade steps are ordered labels, not reading-age or standardized scores |
| How did skill observations change? | First/latest correct divided by total for each skill | Planned report; compare percentage points only with two separate assessments |
| Can staff sustain the routine? | Entry time and feedback from participating staff | Proposed manual collection; no automated workload or satisfaction dashboard |

## Interpret growth carefully

In the institution branch, class skill percentages use summed correct answers
divided by summed question counts. A class mean level change includes only
children with at least two recorded levels, with paired/total coverage shown.
Single observations have no change score; missing data is not zero. Declines
and actual zero scores remain visible. Changes can reflect teaching, differences
in questions, attendance or recording practice; the reports do not isolate the
product's effect.

Before an evaluation, agree the cohort, baseline/end dates, staff entry process,
measures and decision thresholds. Keep the same child cohort for comparisons;
show attrition and avoid averaging unrelated skill percentages. Use the buyer's
existing assessment process for independent evidence. No validated grade-gain,
medical, diagnostic or treatment claim is made. No customer statistics or
testimonials are supplied.

The renewal decision should consider feasibility, completeness, observed activity,
independent educational evidence and total cost. Any success threshold is a
prospective buyer decision, not an achieved result.
