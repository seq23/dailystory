# Library buyer brief

For youth services, branch leaders and library networks serving ages 3-15,
Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the UI has 8 languages.

## Proposed use

Explore facilitated English reading sessions and family orientation alongside
existing library programming. The current family reader provides personalized AI
stories, illustrations, narration, word help and quizzes on a device. Staff can
demonstrate reading features using made-up profiles; local content and
accessibility review should precede a patron program.

## Library-specific limits

There is no library-card authentication, anonymous patron reporting, branch
licensing, circulation integration or library-specific portal in the reviewed
source. **Planned / PR #130** is a school workspace with teacher-entered records,
not a deployed library product. The branch is not merged and does not automatically
ingest family reading activity. Any adaptation to library roles and patron
authority needs a separate scope and readiness review.

Do not share one family account across unrelated patrons. Library participation
does not establish school authority or parental consent. Decide privately who
owns accounts and records, what consent is needed, and how to handle shared-device
sign-out and browser storage. Family reading, voice and AI processing need their
own review; a school workspace's data boundaries do not cover them.

## Proposed scope and budget

Name a youth services sponsor, branch coordinators, technology/privacy reviewers
and an account owner. Define a cohort, supported devices and a supervised
schedule. The [implementation timeline](implementation.md) begins only after the
library operating model and data scope are resolved; library adaptation may
require additional work.

The [pricing proposal](pricing-proposal.md) starts at $10,000/month for an
institutional program and increases with readers and branches. It is likely to
fit a network or sponsored program better than a small standalone branch.
Library-specific entitlements and invoicing are proposed, not implemented.

## Evaluation decision

Agree how staff will record program attendance and collect optional feedback
without exposing patron identities. Attendance and satisfaction collection are
proposed staff processes, not product reports. Use [measurement guidance](outcomes-measurement.md)
without claiming learning efficacy. Review [privacy](privacy-security.md),
[partnership options](partnership.md) and [source evidence](capabilities.md).
