# Proposed implementation timeline

For programs serving ages 3-15, Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the
UI has 8 languages. This is a planning sequence, not a promised delivery date.

## Readiness before the clock starts

The current family reader and **planned / PR #130** institution workspace are
separate. The latter is unmerged at this review. Institution deployment requires
schema, service and frontend release work plus authorized provisioning; code
merge alone does not establish readiness. This sales documentation change does
not authorize or perform those steps or any production database writes.

Library adaptations, consolidated district reporting and institutional billing
are additional scope; the schedule below does not estimate their development.
If required for the buyer, stop scheduling launch until a delivery plan exists.

## Illustrative six-to-eight-week sequence after prerequisites

| Window | Buyer responsibility | Proposed delivery work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weeks 1-2 | Sponsor defines readers, sites, devices, staff and authority; privacy/procurement reviews data scope | Walk through current product and planned workspace with made-up records; agree scope, usage and terms | Written scope; required notices/agreements resolved; feasible support and billing arrangements |
| Week 3 | Technology and program leads confirm access requirements | Separately authorized team verifies deployed workspace, provisions adult roles and validates an alias roster | School/class access boundaries checked; minimal roster accepted; provider connection verified if included |
| Week 4 | Coordinator trains staff and selects evaluation cohort | Practice teacher entry, content review, device/audio access, shared-device sign-out and incident escalation | Staff demonstrate the workflow; accessibility and data-handling issues resolved |
| Weeks 5-6 | Staff run agreed reading sessions and record observations | Review participation, entry completeness and support issues; keep family and school records distinct | Dated evaluation evidence and issue log; no inferred missing baselines |
| Weeks 7-8 | Sponsor reviews cost, workload and educational evidence | Assess continuation, limited expansion or exit against agreed criteria | Buyer decision; agreed data-return/deletion process with completion evidence |

Time can extend for procurement, consent, integration, accessibility or missing
product requirements. Named staff roles above are responsibilities to assign,
not a promise that a staffed implementation team already exists.

## Conditions for a real-data launch

Confirm authority and agreements, deployed access controls, roles, roster accuracy,
supported devices, staff readiness, support ownership and a workable exit process.
Define return/export, correction, retention and deletion procedures before launch;
the planned school workspace does not automate all of them. Do not treat a
configured roster credential as a successful live connection.

Any production rollout needs its own authorized operational change. This packet
does not create accounts, connect providers, send notices or sign agreements.
