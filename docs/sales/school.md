# School buyer brief

For school leaders and reading program coordinators serving ages 3-15,
Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the UI has 8 languages.

## Proposed use

Plan a supervised supplemental reading routine: choose a suitable reading level,
read a personalized story on a device, use word help or narration, and discuss
the quiz. Stories and quizzes are generated with AI; staff should review suitability
and provide instruction. The current family product has adult-owned child profiles
and parent progress views, rather than classroom student logins.

## School workspace: planned / PR #130

The open branch lets assigned teachers manage class rosters and enter actual
reading minutes, completion and dated level/skill observations. A school
administrator can review classes within the school and assign existing adult
accounts as teachers. Initial school/admin provisioning requires an authorized
operator. The workspace is not yet merged or verified in production.

Reading and quiz observations are teacher-entered. Family reading and quizzes do
not automatically appear in the school workspace, and roster import does not
create student sign-in accounts. Include staff entry time in the workload plan.
School export and routine observation-correction workflows remain gaps to resolve.

## Proposed scope and budget

Choose one coordinator, participating staff, eligible readers, devices and a
reading schedule before launch. Follow the [implementation plan](implementation.md)
and [privacy review](privacy-security.md). School authority and notices must be
established before a roster is provisioned.

The [pricing proposal](pricing-proposal.md) has a $10,000/month institutional
minimum. A small school should check whether that managed program scope fits its
budget; a network or sponsored arrangement is a proposal, not an existing shared
license. No seat plan or school purchase-order billing is currently in checkout.

## Evaluation decision

Track participation and staff workload alongside recorded reading minutes and
quiz observations. Use dated evidence and your existing educational judgment;
there is no guaranteed change in reading grade or test scores. See
[measurement limits](outcomes-measurement.md) and [source evidence](capabilities.md).
