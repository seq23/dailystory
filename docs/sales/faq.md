# Institutional buyer FAQ

For ages 3-15, Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the UI has 8 languages.

## What can readers do today?

The current family reader offers personalized AI stories, illustrations, narration,
word help, quizzes and adult progress views. Children are profiles inside adult
accounts. School grade and Reads at can differ. Source presence does not replace
a production acceptance check; see [capability evidence](capabilities.md).

## Does it use screens and AI?

Yes. It is a device-based reading product using AI for story and media features.
Staff should review content and plan adult support. It supplements instruction;
it does not replace teachers or the buyer's curriculum and assessments.

## Are stories available in the UI languages?

Stories are English only. The UI has 8 languages: English, Arabic, Spanish,
Chinese, Hindi, Portuguese, French and Urdu. A translated interface is not a
multilingual story library or evidence of multilingual instructional efficacy.

## Is the institution workspace available now?

**Planned / PR #130:** implemented on an open branch, not merged at this packet's
baseline. It records teacher-entered sessions and assessments, with school/class
access controls. Production deployment and real-school acceptance are unproven.
It does not automatically ingest family reading activity or create student logins.

## Can we import a roster or use SSO?

The institution branch implements CSV preview/import and remote roster adapters.
Connections need existing authorization, server configuration and live testing.
A roster import is not SSO, student account creation, ongoing background sync or
deletion reconciliation. Do not promise those capabilities.

## Is there a district or library dashboard?

No consolidated district dashboard or library-specific portal is included in the
reviewed change. School administrators see their school, not the whole district.
Library roles, patron authentication and shared-device operations need separate
scoping before a program can launch.

## What outcomes are proven?

This packet supplies no customer outcomes, testimonials or efficacy study.
Activity and dated teacher observations can inform evaluation; they do not prove
causal improvement. It makes no medical, diagnostic, treatment or dyslexia claim.
Use the [measurement plan](outcomes-measurement.md) and independent buyer evidence.

## Is the product certified or covered by a school data agreement?

This packet establishes neither certification nor an executed agreement.
Privacy mechanisms and policy pages are not a compliance guarantee. Review
[privacy/security](privacy-security.md) and [docs/privacy/](../privacy/sales-review.md)
for source findings, unresolved requirements and the draft agreement worksheet.

## What does an institution pay?

The [proposal](pricing-proposal.md) starts at $10,000/month and increases with
readers and sites. Institutional billing and entitlements are not implemented.
Commercial scope, allowances, support, invoicing and terms require agreement.
Consumer pricing in [docs/PRICING.md](../PRICING.md) is a separate checkout offer.

## Is premium narration unlimited and guaranteed?

No. Voice access depends on the family plan, configured services and availability;
device speech is the fallback. Institutional narration allowances and capacity
must be scoped before quoting. No unlimited inference or uptime commitment is
included in the proposal.

## Can we export, correct or delete student records?

The institution branch includes authorized school-child deletion with dependent
records. It lacks a school export workflow and routine observation-correction
workflow. Family account exports/deletions do not administer school records.
Agree practical access, correction, retention, backup and termination procedures
before real data; do not promise comprehensive automatic erasure.

## How soon can we start?

Use the conditional [implementation timeline](implementation.md). Begin discovery
with made-up records. Real rosters wait for authorized deployment, operating
procedures and buyer readiness; the illustrative schedule is not a delivery SLA.
