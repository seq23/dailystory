# Partnership one-pager

Proposed institutional and community partnerships serving ages 3-15,
Pre-K-10th grade. Stories are English only; the UI has 8 languages.

## The offer to explore

Support an adult-guided English reading routine across schools, libraries or
after-school sites. The current family reader offers personalized AI stories,
illustrations, narration, word help and quizzes on a device. A partner can help
define the cohort, provide supervision and devices, and sustain the routine.
No existing partner, endorsement or outcome is implied.

## Proposed models

| Model | Partner contribution | Scope that must be agreed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsored institutional access | Fund a defined program and support its coordinator | Eligible cohort, account ownership, usage allowances, term and exit |
| After-school network | Coordinate sites, staff and supervised sessions | Training, records, consent, site responsibilities and support |
| Library/community program | Host orientation and facilitated reading | Patron privacy, account model, shared devices and any library adaptation |

These models are proposals, not existing partner integrations, grant commitments,
reseller rights or automatic licensing programs. Sponsors receive no student data
by default. Any program summary needs agreed aggregation, access and disclosure
rules; there is no sponsor reporting portal in the reviewed source.

## Product and delivery boundary

**Planned / PR #130** adds a school workspace for teacher-entered records and
school-scoped reports. It is not merged or verified in production, does not
automatically ingest family activity, and provides no student sign-in or
institutional billing. Library and after-school roles require their own authority
and operating review; school consent cannot simply be assumed to apply.

Institutional pricing is [proposed from $10,000/month](pricing-proposal.md), with
larger networks paying more. There is no promised revenue share, discount,
referral payment or exclusivity. A written scope must establish obligations,
funding, data terms, permitted communications and support capacity.

## A concrete first discussion

Define sites, eligible readers, devices, supervising adults, funding term and
data responsibilities. Review [privacy](privacy-security.md), set a conditional
[implementation plan](implementation.md), and agree [evaluation measures](outcomes-measurement.md)
before committing to a rollout. This packet initiates no outreach, spending,
account creation or approval. [Source evidence](capabilities.md).
