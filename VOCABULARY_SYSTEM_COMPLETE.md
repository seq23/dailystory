## 🎯 Learning Approach by Level

### **Easy Level (Ages 3-5)**: "Animal Friends" Theme
- Focus: Level 1 vocabulary (sight words, simple concepts)
- Approach: Themed sessions with 5-8 target words per story
- Example: Story about cats, dogs, and birds using basic action words

### **Medium Level (Ages 5-7)**: "Weather Wonders" Theme  
- Focus: Level 2 vocabulary (emotions, weather, body parts)
- Approach: Themed sessions with 8-12 target words per story
- Example: Adventure in different weather using emotion words

### **Hard Level (Ages 7-9)**: Progressive Revelation
- Focus: Advanced vocabulary with gradual input revelation
- Approach: 4-phase revelation (Awakening → Catalyst → Test → Integration)
- Example: Mystery story gradually revealing user's interests and preferences

### **Expert Level (Ages 9+)**: Complex Progressive Revelation
- Focus: Sophisticated vocabulary and concepts
- Approach: 5-phase revelation including philosophical themes
- Example: Multi-layered narrative exploring identity and purpose
